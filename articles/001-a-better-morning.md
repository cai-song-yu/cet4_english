## 一、文章正文

**A Better Morning**

I am not a natural early riser. Last year, my mornings were always <span style="color:#1565C0"><b>rushed</b></span>. Every day I hit the snooze button again and again, got out of bed <span style="color:#2E7D32"><b>at the last minute</b></span>, and ran to the bus stop. Breakfast was often a piece of bread eaten <span style="color:#2E7D32"><b>on the way</b></span>.

I told myself that I could not <span style="color:#1565C0"><b>afford</b></span> to spend twenty minutes on breakfast, because my classes started at eight. <span style="color:#C62828">The result was that I felt tired all morning and could not pay attention in class.</span>

One day, my roommate asked me a simple question: "Do you really have no time, or do you just not <span style="color:#2E7D32"><b>make time</b></span>?" The question <span style="color:#2E7D32"><b>stayed with</b></span> me for a long time.

The next week, I made a small <span style="color:#1565C0"><b>change</b></span>. I began to wake up twenty minutes <span style="color:#1565C0"><b>extra</b></span> early. I wash my face, drink a glass of water, and eat breakfast at the table. This simple change costs nothing and <span style="color:#1565C0"><b>requires</b></span> no special skill, but it brought two clear <span style="color:#1565C0"><b>benefits</b></span>.

First, I am <span style="color:#6A1B9A"><b>no longer in a hurry</b></span>, so class begins with a calm mind. Second, I found that a good morning gives the whole day better <span style="color:#1565C0"><b>quality</b></span>.

<span style="color:#E65100"><b>I learned that</b></span> a good day does not begin at eight o'clock. It begins twenty minutes earlier.

## 二、核心词汇

| Word | 中文 | 常见搭配（含意思） | 例句 |
|---|---|---|---|

| **change** /tʃeɪndʒ/ | 改变、变化 | make a change — 做出改变；change one's habit — 改变习惯 | She made one small change to her diet. |
| **extra** /ˈekstrə/ | 额外的 | extra time — 额外的时间；twenty minutes extra — 多二十分钟 | I need some extra time to finish it. |
| **require** /rɪˈkwaɪə(r)/ | 需要、要求 | require sth. — 需要某物；require sb. to do — 要求某人做 | The job requires a lot of patience. |
| **benefit** /ˈbenɪfɪt/ | 好处、益处 | bring benefits — 带来好处；benefit from — 从……中受益 | Exercise brings many benefits. |

## 四、写作表达

**1. <span style="color:#E65100">The result was that ...</span>**

- **中文**：其结果是……
- **含义**：引出某个行为造成的**结果**，是四级写作连接"原因—结果"最常用的句式。
- **使用语境**：先写一个行为或问题，再写它的后果。适合现象分析、图画作文的原因段。
- **替换表达**：
  - `As a result, ...` — 后接完整句子，更简洁
  - `... which led to ...` — 更书面
  - `Consequently, ...` — 正式，阶段五以后常用
- **正式程度**：中性，写作口语都能用。
- **例句**：I stayed up late every night. The result was that I could not focus in class.

**2. <span style="color:#E65100">I learned that ...</span>**

- **中文**：我认识到…… / 我明白了……
- **含义**：用于结尾段总结个人感悟或道理。
- **使用语境**：记叙文、议论文的最后一段，点出全文的道理。
- **替换表达**：
  - `From this experience, I understand that ...`
  - `It occurred to me that ...` — 更正式
  - `Only then did I realize that ...` — 倒装句，阶段四以后用
- **正式程度**：中性偏个人化。
- **例句**：I learned that small habits are more powerful than big plans.

## 五、重点句子解析

> <span style="color:#C62828">**The result was that I felt tired all morning and could not pay attention in class.**</span>

- **主干**：The result was ...（主语 + 系动词 + 表语）
- **表语从句**：`that I felt tired all morning and could not pay attention in class` —— 整个 that 从句作表语，说明"结果"具体是什么。这是 **that 引导表语从句**的典型结构。
- **从句内部**：`felt tired` 与 `could not pay attention` 由 and 并列，两个谓语共用一个从句。
- **为什么值得学**：四级写作最实用的"因果链"——先写行为，再用 The result was that ... 引出后果，逻辑立刻清晰。注意**表语从句的 that 一般不可省略**。
- **语法提醒**：pay attention 后不直接接宾语，要说 pay attention **to** sth.

## 九、本篇学习重点

1. **新授核心词 8 个**：rushed, afford, attention, change, extra, require, benefit, quality —— 全是四级高频词，**afford / attention / benefit / quality 会在后续文章反复复现**，记搭配而非只记中文。
2. **因果表达两件套**：`because ...`（句内原因）+ `The result was that ...`（引出结果）。
3. **动词搭配积累**：get out of bed、wake up、make time、pay attention、make a change —— 这类"动词+名词"搭配是口语和写作的骨架，比单个单词更常用。
4. **全文逻辑线**：过去（匆忙）→ 转折点（一句话）→ 改变（早起）→ 收获（两个好处）→ 感悟。这是最基础的英语记叙文结构，后面会不断升级复用。
5. **阅读策略**：第 3、5 题需要稍微动脑推断——读文章时先问自己"作者为什么写这一句"，而不是急着翻译。
